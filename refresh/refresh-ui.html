00

The 30-second snapshot

What Oil India is, in one screen

Oil India is a Navratna upstream explorer-producer: it finds and pumps crude oil and natural gas, so its profits track the global crude price almost one-for-one.

Unlike the pure explorers, it owns ~69.6% of Numaligarh Refinery (NRL) — a downstream hedge that earns refining margins when crude is high and upstream realisations soften.

02

The crude-price driver

Why Brent is the master variable

Every dollar on Brent flows almost directly to Oil India's realisation and profit — the single biggest swing factor in the story.

FY26 realisation averaged ~$69/bbl versus ~$78 the year before, which is most of the year-on-year earnings move.

03

The NRL refinery leg

The 3→9 MTPA expansion that hedges the cycle

NRL is the crown jewel — its tripling to 9 MTPA is the biggest growth leg, adding a refining-margin stream that cushions the upstream cycle.

The ~₹34,000 Cr project has been the key thing to watch for commissioning timing.
